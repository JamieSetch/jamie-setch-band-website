About Me

Hi, I'm Jamie Setch, an independent musician and NeuroAI Researcher at the University of Cambridge.

My team and I recently launched the nerve initiative (nerveinitiative.com), where we continue to build an infrastructure for biomimetic robots. Life has been hectic, some of the text on my website may be out of date, but balancing my music and academic career is my higher calling.
